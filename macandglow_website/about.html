Our Story

Science Meets Nature

Founded in 2026 in Noida, Mac & Glow was born from a simple belief: beautiful skin shouldn't require compromises.

How It Started

A Passion Project Turned Movement

Mac & Glow began in a Noida apartment in 2026, when founder Mr. Jay Srivastava grew frustrated with the gap between what was available in the Indian market and what people actually needed.

He partnered with experts to create formulas that combined the best of clinical science with India's rich tradition of plant-based healing. The result was a range that worked for Indian skin — in Indian weather, across all Indian skin tones.

Today, Mac & Glow serves over 25,000 customers across India and is proudly formulated, manufactured, and shipped from our facility in Pune.
